# Phase 34 — Oxanium Typography

## Implementation

LifeLink now loads Oxanium from Google Fonts and applies it to the global body text, heading hierarchy, and compact mobile brand treatment. The existing sizing scale and visual contrast tokens are preserved, while the new line-height and heading tracking keep the more geometric letterforms readable.

## Mobile visual review

At 390 × 844, the login and authenticated patient dashboard retain a clear visual hierarchy. Headings remain distinct from labels and body copy, form controls remain legible, and the dashboard cards continue to fit the viewport without clipping or horizontal overflow.

At 1440 × 900, the login card, sidebar navigation, dashboard headings, care-summary cards, and Quick Actions panel retain balanced spacing and legible contrast. The Oxanium treatment is visible throughout without changing protected patient workflows or responsive layout behavior.
